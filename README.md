# Palm Engineering

Professional precision CNC machining and manufacturing services in Gqeberha, South Africa.

## About Us

Palm Engineering specializes in high-precision CNC machining and manufacturing solutions for demanding industries. With over 25 years of experience, we deliver exceptional quality components with tolerances up to ±0.01mm.

## Our Services

### CNC Milling
High-precision 3, 4 & 5-axis CNC milling for complex parts with tight tolerances.

### CNC Turning
Advanced lathe work for cylindrical components and precision rotational parts.

### Precision Grinding
Surface and cylindrical grinding achieving superior surface finishes.

### Wire EDM
Electrical discharge machining for intricate profiles in hardened materials.

### Quality Control
CMM inspection and comprehensive quality assurance documentation.

### Prototyping
Rapid prototyping services from engineering concept to finished component.
